Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: location background for Reversal City, a browser exploration game for high-school students. Original environmental illustration only, no game UI.
Primary request: A clean sunlit branching transit station in an elegant seaside campus city, named Conditional Station only in the game outside the artwork. Warm ivory stone station architecture with beautiful open arches and a muted teal barrel-vault roof, pale apricot roof details. Two very clear transit route corridors lead through separate simple entrance gates: the LEFT route has a broad soft APRICOT surface between thin cream-and-brass rails; the RIGHT route has a broad muted TEAL surface between matching rails. The routes separate naturally in an airy spacious station courtyard, each gate structurally clear and completely unlettered. Show the route split prominently, as a quiet architectural metaphor for conditional branching. A perfectly blank cream signboard is suspended above each of the two entrance gates. Sage-green plants, a few benches and restrained brass station fixtures at the outer edges, with the sky-blue sea and distant ivory campus architecture visible through open sides.
Composition/framing: Horizontal landscape 1536x1024. Elevated three-quarter 2.5D camera. Clear foreground paths leading the eye toward the two station entries. Architecture across the back half, space between the two colored track corridors and calm open cream paving through the middle foreground suitable for interaction overlays. No people and no trains obstructing the gates, no crowded machinery.
Style/medium: Sophisticated original animated-feature environment art with real architectural proportions, delicate painterly texture, crisp readable shapes and soft fine shadows. Elegant cream-apricot-sage-blue coastal campus aesthetic for teenagers. Not a toy model.
Lighting/mood: Clear natural morning sun, warm limestone highlights, soft blue ambient shadows, calm and curious.
Color palette/materials: Warm ivory limestone and pale plaster, pale apricot tile and left track, muted teal roof and right track, sage foliage, sky-blue sky and water, restrained brushed brass. Matte surfaces, subtle microtexture.
Text: Absolutely no letters, numbers, formulae, logos, symbols, watermarks, or signatures. All signs and boards must remain completely blank.
Avoid: neon, pixel art, cyberpunk, baby toys, glossy plastics, optical prisms, glass cards, floating UI elements, collage, thick outlines. One continuous beautiful scene.
